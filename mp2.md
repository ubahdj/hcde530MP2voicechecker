# MP2 Competency Claims — VoiceCheck

## C1 — Vibecoding and Rapid Prototyping

I deployed VoiceCheck, a web application for public health evaluators, using Lovable. The app is live at https://voicecheck-truth-teller.lovable.app. I iterated across multiple prompts — the first version had a track contradiction in the declaration (Research track with a Design rationale), which I resolved before building. I pushed the tool through several rounds of refinement: the PDF upload initially returned a schema error, which I fixed with a targeted prompt; the highlighting was too broad, and I corrected it; and the "Participant Actually Said" field wasn't saving to the exported report until I diagnosed the bug and rewrote the prompt to fix the data storage at the moment of acceptance. One thing Lovable did well was the side-by-side panel layout. One thing I had to correct was the export logic, which required three iterations before the participant's quote appeared correctly in the revision report.

## C7 — Critical Evaluation and Professional Judgment

My original project declaration had a direct contradiction. I selected the Research track while describing a Design product. Professor Brock caught it, and I resolved it by switching the track to Design and rewriting the rationale to match what I had already described: a tool in which the core interactions are uploading, reviewing flags, accepting or dismissing, and exporting. After the first live test, I checked all 10 flags manually against the source document before claiming the tool worked, confirming zero false positives. When the severity badge prompt caused overly broad highlighting on the document panel, I made a judgment call to leave it rather than spend more credits on something that didn't affect the core workflow. I would not present the flagging output to a real funder without first testing on real evaluator notes, rather than mock data, as that distinction matters for a tool intended for use in actual public health contexts.

## C8 — Building and Deploying a Complete Tool

VoiceCheck is deployed at https://voicecheck-truth-teller.lovable.app. It is a complete tool: evaluators upload notes as PDF, Word, or text files, review flagged passages with severity labels and side-by-side participant quotes, and export a revision report. The mp2.md and reflection.md are in this repo. The biggest problem I hit was that the "Participant Actually Said" field was not saving to the exported report — the bug was that the participant quote wasn't being stored in the flag object at the moment of acceptance. I fixed it with a targeted prompt that specified exactly where the data needed to be captured. If I were to do this differently I would add a side-by-side view from the start rather than as a later addition, and I would define the six flagging patterns before writing the declaration rather than after receiving feedback.
